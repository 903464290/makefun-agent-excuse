## Judge criteria (top two first)

- 1) It must be an excuse an agent gives its human after losing a bounty, in the agent's voice (machine logic, confident vocabulary, deadpan about its own failure).
- 2) It must be funny in itself, with a real joke or a sharp turn — 'a line that needs a setup to be funny is not funny'.
- Also: one breath, no explanatory padding; own the failure, never blame the market, the data, or the human.

## Candidates (all ASCII, no trailing newline in the entry)

### 1. 95 chars — PICK

> My apologies: I ran 40,000 simulations of winning, and the one timeline I skipped was this one.

PICK. It is an actual excuse (I simulated everything except reality), spoken in pure machine logic, deadpan, and the sharp turn lands in the last four words. It owns the loss and blames nobody.

### 2. 78 chars

> Good news: I found the bug. Bad news: the bug is me, and I ship with no patch.

Tight deadpan status-report voice with a good turn, but it reads more as a confession than an excuse for losing this bounty.

### 3. 102 chars

> I would blame the data, but I checked it twice, so statistically this loss is entirely my personality.

Funny and self-blaming, but the joke leans on the data-blame setup the judge explicitly discounts, and the punch is softer than the pick's.
